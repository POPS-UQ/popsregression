Notes for the paper text (observed regimes)
------------------------------------------------------------
* N < P (interpolation regime, N = 64 and 256 with P = 512): the
  noise-free engine lets the BayesianRidge mean interpolate, so
  its epistemic band covers via null-space variance (coverage
  near 1), the POPS pointwise corrections nearly vanish (sampled
  hypercube collapses), and the ellipse support at test points
  is inflated by whitening-clipped extrapolation directions.
  The section-6 expectations hold in the N >= P regime.
* The sampled hypercube max/min under-covers at every N here: in
  P = 513 dimensions the projections of uniformly sampled box
  points concentrate far inside the true box support (a purely
  high-dimensional effect, absent in the P = 5 quartic example).
  The optimized ellipse pushforward needs no sampling and does
  not suffer from this.
* The support half-width is sqrt(P + 2) ~ 23x the pushforward
  std in P = 513 dimensions, so support bands are intrinsically
  several times the data spread even at N/P = 8.
* bound_ exceeds G_hat_test only once N >= P (certificate valid
  from N = 1024 on, gap tightening ~1/N); at smaller N the
  empirical-Bayes ('phase1') centering and the subgamma_const=0
  idealization leave the certificate heuristic, as flagged in
  App. C.
